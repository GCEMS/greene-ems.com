Service area

Where we are,
and who we cover.

Six paramedic units out of five stations, placed so that every town in Greene County has advanced life support within reach. Neighboring counties can call on us too.

Town boundaries are simplified. Athens, Durham and New Baltimore are split between units by fire district; see the station list below.
Primary ALS coverage

Ashland · Athens · Cairo · Coxsackie · Durham · Greenville · Hunter · Jewett · Lexington · New Baltimore · Prattsville

Mutual-aid ALS coverage

Catskill · Windham

Mutual aid

Out-of-county ALS mutual aid to Albany, Columbia and Schoharie counties.

How dispatch works Call 911. Greene County 911 dispatches your local ambulance and the nearest available paramedic unit at the same time. You never have to ask for us.
Where we live

The Five Stations

One paramedic unit in each, staffed around the clock, from the river towns to the mountaintop.
MEDIC 3Cairo Paramedic StationHeadquarters
93A County Route 84, Cairo, NY 12413 Primary territory: Cairo, Athens (Leeds Fire District), and Durham southwest of Route 145. Mutual-aid ALS to Catskill. Also home to Medic 1, the Chief of Operations' vehicle. It is a fully equipped sixth unit with the same kit as the station trucks, and it responds to calls and backs up daily operations countywide as needed.
MEDIC 5Coxsackie
Town of Coxsackie Ambulance · 117 Mansion St., Coxsackie, NY 12051 Primary territory: Coxsackie, Athens (Athens and West Athens Fire Districts), and New Baltimore (New Baltimore Fire District).
MEDIC 7Freehold
Freehold Fire Department · 9502 State Route 32, Freehold, NY 12083 Primary territory: Greenville, Durham northeast of Route 145, and New Baltimore (Medway-Grapeville Fire District).
MEDIC 8Prattsville
Prattsville Hose Company · 47 Firehouse Dr., Prattsville, NY 12468 Primary territory: Ashland and Prattsville, the most rural corner of the county, where transports routinely run an hour or more. Backs up Medic 9 in Lexington.
MEDIC 9Hunter
7991 Main St., Hunter, NY 12442 Primary territory: Hunter, Jewett and Lexington. Mutual-aid ALS to Windham.
