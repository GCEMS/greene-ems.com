In every town

What arrives when you call

1

Two units are sent

Greene County 911 dispatches your local ambulance and a Greene County paramedic at the same time.

2

The paramedic comes to you

The paramedic arrives in a fly car with a cardiac monitor, a ventilator, and every medication a New York paramedic can carry.

3

Care continues on the ride

When you need a paramedic, ours rides in the ambulance with you to the hospital.

Whichever unit is closest and available responds. Station lines are a starting point, not a boundary.
