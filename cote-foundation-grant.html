Grant award · 2026

$44,400 from the Berthe M. Cote Foundation

A grant that will put new critical-care equipment on every Greene County Paramedic fly car.

How it came about

A foundation that invests in rural care, and an agency with a plan

The Berthe M. Cote Foundation supports health care in our corner of New York. In early 2026, Chief of Operations Steve Near wrote and submitted an application with a specific proposal: equipment that would let our paramedics do more for the sickest patients on the longest transports in the county. The Foundation approved it in April 2026.

The Foundation awarded $44,400, the full amount requested, to purchase and deploy that equipment across every fly car.

How we'll use it

The grant funds a new critical-care capability that our Medical Director has approved and that we are building now: equipment, protocols, training, and the quality review to go with it. We are not announcing the details until the program is ready to go in service. When it is, you will read about it here first.

What we can say is that it is aimed at the problem that defines EMS in Greene County: the distance between where people get hurt and where a hospital can help them.
