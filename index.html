The fly car system

You call 911.
We're already
moving.

Your local ambulance responds — and so does a Greene County Paramedic, in a rapid-response SUV carrying every medication and intervention New York State allows a paramedic to provide, plus the monitors, infusion pumps and ventilators usually found on a critical-care transport. We start advanced care where you are, then ride with the ambulance all the way to the hospital.

Every call we run is a 911 call — we don't do inter-facility transfers. It's a model built for a county of mountains and long roads, and we've been refining it since August 1, 2000.

Capabilities

Not your average

Equipment and protocols built for long transports with sick patients. Here's some of what rides with us.

Flagship program An ICU-worthy ventilator, in every car The Hamilton T1 — the same ventilator used in critical-care transport — rides in every fly car. Three years in, read the review.
Precision infusions Hospital-grade pumps that run our medications as exact infusions, from minute one to the hospital door.
Every ALS medication The full New York State paramedic formulary, on every truck, every shift.
Technical rescue Rescue Paramedics trained for the backcountry, high-angle and swift-water terrain of the Catskills.
